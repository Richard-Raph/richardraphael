Since 1995

Three decades of design and build expertise, concentrated on the hardest structures in West Africa's built environment.

ITB Nigeria Limited was established to meet a growing demand for genuine engineering rigor in Nigerian construction. Today we are the contractor developers call when a project is technically difficult, structurally ambitious, or simply too important to get wrong — from waterfront towers in Eko Atlantic to institutional and industrial work across the region.

Every division, from design through concrete, precast, post-tensioning and fit-out, sits under one roof. That's how we hold a single standard from foundation to façade.
